# Kalpane privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026.

Kalpane is a note-taking app. This policy explains what happens to your information.

## What stays on your device
Your notebooks, pages, drawings, images and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. The developer does not receive them.

## GitHub remote (optional)
If you add a GitHub repository as a remote, Kalpane sends and receives your notebook files directly between your device and GitHub (api.github.com) when you pull or push, or when Auto push runs. This uses a personal access token that you create on GitHub. The token is encrypted with the Android Keystore and is only sent to api.github.com. Your notes are then subject to GitHub's terms and privacy statement. You can remove the remote at any time in Settings; files already in your repository stay there until you delete them on GitHub.

## Images
Images you insert are chosen with the Android photo picker and copied into the app. Kalpane does not request access to your photo library.

## No tracking
Kalpane has no ads, analytics or tracking, and no account with the developer. If the app crashes, a crash report is saved on your device only; you can view, copy or delete it in Settings.

## Sharing and export
Exported PDFs and images are shared only when you choose to, through the Android share sheet.

## Deleting your data
Deleting a page or notebook moves it to Trash, which is emptied after 30 days or when you choose. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data.

## Children
Kalpane is not directed at children.
